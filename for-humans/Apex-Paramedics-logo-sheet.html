05What to watch

  • Apex Gold #FDB61C is never text on white. It measures 1.77:1 and cannot be read. Use it as a rule, an accent mark, or a numeral on the dark blue band, where it measures 8.24:1.
  • Gold text on white is Apex Gold Deep #B77808, and only large. It measures 3.68:1. 18pt in print or 24px on screen and up. Never a caption, a table cell or body copy.
  • Apex Blue Light #9699C7 is never text and never a large background. It measures 2.74:1 on white.
  • White text belongs only on Apex Blue or Apex Blue Deep.
  • Tertiary #6D6D77 is the lightest gray that carries text. Dimmed #8A8B93 is never text, at any size.
  • On a dark band, headings are white #FFFFFF, body text is #DDDDE3 and captions are #A3A4AC.
  • These colors are for the brand. Chart colors are a separate set. Open data-color-sheet.html.

06Type

Montserrat throughout. There is no secondary typeface and no italic. Four weights: Regular 400, SemiBold 600, Bold 700, ExtraBold 800. Three at most in one piece. The four weights are in fonts/static/, and the variable file is in fonts/.

07Do not

  • Stretch, squash, skew or rotate it.
  • Recolor it. Pick the file that matches the background instead.
  • Change the letters to anything but Apex Blue #2B338D or white, or the arc to anything but Apex Gold #FDB61C.
  • Render the arc in blue or the letters in gold.
  • Pull the letters apart from the arc, or use the arc on its own. The arc alone is not an Apex logo.
  • Redraw the A mark. Use the file.
  • Use the compact lockup where the horizontal one fits.
  • Add a drop shadow, glow, outline or gradient.
  • Put it on a busy photo without a solid backing panel behind it.
  • Crop the clear space to make it fit. Shrink the logo instead, down to the minimum.
  • Use it below the minimum sizes above.
  • Merge it with another logo into one shape.
