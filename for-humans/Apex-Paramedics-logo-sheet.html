Three traps, all measured

  • Apex Gold #FDB61C is never text on white. It measures 1.77:1. It is a rule, an accent mark, or a numeral on a dark blue band.
  • Gold text on white is Apex Gold Deep #B77808, and only at 18pt in print or 24px on screen and larger.
  • Apex Blue Light #9699C7 is never text and never a large background. On a dark blue band use #D6DAEC for body text and #9EA5C4 for captions.

Do not

  • Stretch, squash, skew or rotate the logo.
  • Recolor it. Pick the file that matches the background.
  • Render the arc in blue or the letters in gold.
  • Add a drop shadow, glow, outline or gradient.
  • Separate the letters from the arc, or use the arc on its own.
  • Use the compact lockup where the horizontal one fits.
  • Place it on a busy photograph without a solid backing panel.
  • Render it below the minimum sizes above.
