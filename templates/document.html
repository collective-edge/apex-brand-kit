Two added night units remove crew overtime.

Night call volume at Station 4 has run above plan for six weeks. Apex adds two ALS units between 19:00 and 07:00 from 1 December.

Summary.

Night calls in the Station 4 area rose through October. Median response held inside the contracted window, and the crews paid for it in overtime: two crews worked past the end of shift on 11 of the last 14 nights.

Two added ALS units at Station 4 take the extra calls. No day crew moves and no day unit covers a night post.

Where the calls come from.

Most of the added calls start within two miles of Station 4. Posting both units there keeps drive time short and leaves Station 2 coverage as it is.

Risks.

Medic 42 is pending its inspection, booked for 26 November. Until it clears, Medic 17 covers the second night post from Station 2.

If the inspection date moves, Fleet calls the night supervisor the same day and Medic 17 stays at Station 4.

Next steps.

  1. 1Supervisors confirm night crew assignments by 24 November.
  2. 2Fleet completes the Medic 42 inspection on 26 November.
  3. 3Medic 41 and Medic 42 post at Station 4 from the night of 1 December.
  4. 4Operations reviews night overtime again on 15 January 2027.

Method.

A night call is any call dispatched between 19:00 and 07:00 to an address in the Station 4 area. Plan is the weekly figure in the 2026 staffing model. An overtime night is a night on which a crew clocked out more than 30 minutes after the end of shift.

Counts come from dispatch records and the payroll export for the same weeks. Calls handed to a mutual aid partner are counted once, under the unit first assigned.

Limits.

Six weeks does not show a season. The January review covers the holiday period and the first full month with both units posted.
