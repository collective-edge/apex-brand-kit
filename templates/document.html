Operations brief

Interfacility performance, August 2026

Apex ran 1,486 interfacility transports in August across 22 origin facilities. Two thirds moved a patient from an acute floor to a skilled nursing bed. The rest split between dialysis rounds, imaging appointments off campus, and hospital to hospital transfers that required a critical care team.

Activation time is the number partners feel. It runs from bed offer accepted to wheels rolling at the origin door. Median activation held at 38 minutes countywide. The slowest quartile sat at 71 minutes, and every case in it traced to one of three causes: paperwork not ready on the floor, no gurney unit inside the county, or a receiving bed that was never confirmed.
