Apex Paramedics · Interfacility transport 02

Activation

Activation time is the number partners feel.

Activation runs from bed offer accepted to wheels rolling at the origin door. It is the only interval a case manager watches, and it is the one Apex controls end to end. Median activation held at 38 minutes countywide in August.

The slowest quartile sat at 71 minutes. Every case in it traced to one of three causes: paperwork not ready on the floor, no gurney unit inside the county, or a receiving bed that was never confirmed. Two of the three are fixed at the origin, not in the truck.
